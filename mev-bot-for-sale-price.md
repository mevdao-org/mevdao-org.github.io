# An MEV bot for sale, and what the price tag tells you

Price is the first tell. If it made money every block, why would anyone sell it? They'd just run it. Sell it to ten people and those ten bots fight for the same trade, so whatever edge it had is gone pretty quick. Cheap and "profitable" don't really go together.

Next, what are you actually buying. Source you can read and run on your own node? Or just an address you send ETH into? The second is the old trick. ETH goes in, only the deployer can take it out. We've opened tons of these and the owner check is always tucked in some helper with a boring name.
