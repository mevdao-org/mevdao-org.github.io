# Is a market making bot the same as a volume bot?

is a market making bot the same as a volume bot? no, and token teams mix them up a lot.

market making means a bid below price and an ask above it. if both fill you keep the spread. if only one fills you're stuck holding bags you didnt want, so you lean the quotes. that part is the hard bit.

volume bot is the bot trading with itself so the chart looks busy. nobody else gets a fill. thats wash trading, which gets prosecuted.

on uniswap you dont post quotes. you pick a price range and arb bots take the stale price off you, people call it LVR.

so if someone sells you "market making", ask who's actually on the other side of the trades.
