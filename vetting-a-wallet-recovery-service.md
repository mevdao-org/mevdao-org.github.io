# Questions we'd ask a hacked wallet recovery service

Lots of people who pay a "recovery agent" after a hack get hit twice. Some of these services are real, though, and we're one of them, so here's what we'd want someone to ask us.

First thing we'd ask is what they think they can get. Whatever is still in the address, sure. Tokens, NFTs, staked stuff, an airdrop. But the ETH the bot already swept is on someone else's wallet now and it's not coming back. If they tell you otherwise and want a fee to "trace" it, hang up.

Also, did you go looking for them or did they show up in your replies? The fake ones sit on X and Reddit waiting for hack posts. An hour after you vent about it, there they are.

Then the seed. Some real rescues need the hacked key to sign. The good ones explain why and keep it to the signing step, and the key is burned after. Anyone asking for it in a web form or a Telegram chat with no explanation is not someone to hand it to.

## Money

Someone wants a "gas deposit" or "unlock fee" sent to a wallet they give you? Nope. Gas for a rescue gets paid from your own clean wallet. We put our price on our site, with the refund terms, and you can read it before you ever talk to us.
