# EIP-7702 or ERC-4337? Depends what you want to keep

Got asked this twice this week so writing it down. 7702 or 4337? Depends what you want to keep.

4337 is pretty much a new wallet. Contract account, new address, your txs go through bundlers and the EntryPoint instead of the normal mempool. You get batching, gas in USDC, recovery that isn't one seed phrase. You lose your old address though, so ENS, old approvals, airdrop history, all of that stays behind.

7702 is your old address in a smart account costume. You sign an authorization, the EOA points at some contract code, done. Same address. But the old key is still the boss, and those authorizations are what drainer sites fish for now. Sign the wrong one and your address runs somebody else's code.

Like your address? 7702. Scared of your seed phrase? 4337. Lots of wallets will end up doing both anyway lol.

And if theres a 7702 authorization on your wallet you dont remember signing, go check that first. Seriously.
