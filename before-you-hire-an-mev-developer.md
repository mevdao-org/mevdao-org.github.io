# Before you hire an MEV bot developer

We take these calls, so here's the stuff that makes them go well. And the stuff that tells you to hang up.

## Write the strategy down first

One paragraph. Arbitrage between which pools, liquidations on which protocol, backruns on what. "A profitable MEV bot" isn't a spec, and anyone who takes that brief and quotes a price is guessing.

## Know your capital and your chain

Arb bots on mainnet live or die on gas and inventory. A bot that's fine with 50 ETH can lose money with 2. Say how much you can put behind it and on which chain, upfront.

## Ask who holds the keys

The bot signs with a hot key. It should be yours, generated by you, on infra you control. A developer who wants to run it on their server with their key is running your money.

## Walk away if

They promise a daily return. They show you a dashboard of profits they won't let you check on chain. They send a Remix contract and tell you to fund it. That last one is the [Remix MEV bot scam](https://mevdao.org/blog/the-youtube-mev-bot-scam-how-the-remix-contract-drains-your-eth) and it's still everywhere.
