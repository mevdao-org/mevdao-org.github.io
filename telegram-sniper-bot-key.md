# Before you paste a key into a Telegram sniper bot

"Which Telegram bot is safe?" comes up in our DMs a lot. Honestly we never have a clean answer. What we ask back is: if the bot gets popped next Tuesday, how much of yours goes with it?

First thing, who has the key. Most of these bots generate a wallet for you and keep the key on their box, that's how they buy in the same block without you signing anything. Fine, but a breach on their end hits every user at once. It's happened. And please don't import your main wallet's key into one, ever. Spin up a new one.

Second, how much is sitting in there tonight. We've talked to folks who left their whole bag on the bot wallet for months because withdrawing was annoying. Top it up before a launch, pull profits after. Boring, works.

Third, the fee on the site isn't the whole bill. Priority fee and builder tip get stacked on top, and on a hot launch the tip alone can beat the bot's own cut. If it won't show you the tip it set, you can't tell a bad fill from a bad setting.
